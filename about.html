Our Story
About Club

We grew from a single hike to Issyk-Kul

In 2014, three friends led a group of acquaintances to the alpine lake for the first time. Since then, "Steppe & Mountains" has grown into a club where we walk every route ourselves before guiding guests through it.

We believe that Central Asia cannot be "seen from a bus window": it must be lived — in a yurt, at a dastarkhan, on the trail.

Our Principles

Four rules we follow on every tour

  • Small Groups No more than 10 people: this maintains the atmosphere and route flexibility.
  • Local Teams Guides, cooks, and drivers are locals; the money stays in the region.
  • Safety Satellite communication, first aid kits, and insurance are included in every tour.
  • Careful Travel We carry out all trash and support eco-initiatives in the mountains.
